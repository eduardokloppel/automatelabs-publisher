AutomateLabs Publisher

Internal publishing tool by AutomateLabs (Comexify Consultoria LTDA), Brazil.

What this application does

AutomateLabs Publisher is an internal tool that our team uses to upload our own marketing videos to our own TikTok account. Videos are produced in our internal video pipeline, then sent to TikTok as drafts so a member of our team can review and publish them from the TikTok app.

It is not a public product. There is no sign up, and it serves only accounts owned by AutomateLabs and its founder.

How the TikTok integration is used

  • Login Kit (user.info.basic): to authenticate the account owner and confirm which TikTok account will receive the upload.
  • Content Posting API (video.upload): to send a finished video file to that account's drafts. Publishing is completed by a human inside TikTok.
